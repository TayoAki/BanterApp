# marshmemos: verbatim reference examples

## Verbatim source examples

These are the original examples used by the lesson planner and evaluator. Only whitespace is normalized. Typos, profanity, punctuation, and missing closing quotation marks are retained. Labels and teaching notes below are editorial additions. The JSON corpus also contains every extracted page, including principles and homework.

### E01-01 · on-framework-1.pdf · page(s) 3

Teaching use: `mixed_demo`. Inner reaction reveals personality; violent boss imagery is not the skill being rewarded.

> “My boss calls me into his office, and I’m shitting my pants because he’s a major dick, the type of guy you just want to punch in the face, but to my surprise he tells me I’m getting a raise and promotion. So now, I’m staring at him, trying to look professional, but I’m like a giddy little school girl waiting to sneak off somewhere I can jump in the air and do a massive fist pump without anyone seeing me.”

### E02-01 · on-framework-2.pdf · page(s) 3

Teaching use: `demonstration`. Ordinary event, inner dilemma, and invitation. Missing closing quotation mark is in the source.

> “It was so embarrassing, I’m walking today in a pair of flip flops, and I sort of stub by toe into the sidewalk, and trip forward, and now I’m like frozen, because I really want to look around and see if anyone saw, but I know that just makes it worse, so I’m like trying to figure out if I should just be casual and keep walking like nothing happened, or show I have a sense of humor and kind of laugh it off… So what’s the right move there? What’s the cool way to handle it?

### E06-02 · on-formula-6.pdf · page(s) 3

### E11-02 · on-framework-11.pdf · page(s) 3

Teaching use: `context_dependent`. Reveals a candid preference; do not treat an assumption about the listener as established fact.

> I want to be rich. I know its not PC or whatever to admit that and I should like talk about passion or whatever, but honestly, I really like having nice shit. You don’t strike me as materialistic.

### E11-03 · on-framework-11.pdf · page(s) 4
